## Question
1. In Lab 1 you used the vendor's published checksum to prove that `invoice-resent.txt` was a forgery. In Lab 3 you signed `message.txt` and saw verification fail against `forged-message.txt`. What does a digital signature prove that a published SHA-256 checksum alone cannot?

## Options
Option 1: That the file has not been altered, which a checksum cannot detect at all
Option 2: That the file came from the holder of a specific private key, because an attacker who alters the file cannot produce a matching signature without that key
Option 3: That the file is encrypted, so its contents stay confidential in transit
Option 4: That the file is smaller than 245 bytes and therefore safe to transmit

## Answers
Option 2 : 1
